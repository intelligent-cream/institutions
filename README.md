# institutions

Data and visualisations on how well institutions hold up against concentrated wealth.

## Contents

- [`data/tax-at-the-top/`](data/tax-at-the-top/) — effective tax rates across the income distribution in the Netherlands, France, Italy and the United States, and where they turn down for the richest. Dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/Vgb4Uiqt4haGYxJcCwzzZf

- [`analysis/missing_revenue.py`](analysis/missing_revenue.py) — estimates the tax missed because the top 1% pay lower rates than high earners just below them. Run `python3 analysis/missing_revenue.py`.
